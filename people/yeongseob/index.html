Research Interests

My research focuses on statistical methodology and machine learning for spatial, spatiotemporal, network-structured, and point process data, with particular interests in the areas listed below. If you are interested in discussing these or related topics, please feel free to contact me by email.

  • Empirical Likelihood Methods
  • Machine Learning for Spatial, Spatiotemporal, and Point Process Data
  • Graph Neural Networks for Spatial Data
  • Generative Modeling for Point Pattern Data
