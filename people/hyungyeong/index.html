Research Interests

My research focuses on graphical models, particularly on developing graph neural network architectures for modeling sports data. My current work explores the use of graph neural networks and reinforcement learning to model and analyze strategic decision making in baseball. I am also interested in point processes and spatiotemporal data analysis. I am open to a wide range of machine learning and deep learning methodologies, and I enjoy brainstorming new ways to apply these methods to real-world data and problems.
